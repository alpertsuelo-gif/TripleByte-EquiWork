# EquiWork
### A Java-Based Informal Job Posting and Matching System

---

## 1. Project Description

EquiWork is a web-based job-posting and matching platform developed using Java. It aims to make informal and short-term employment opportunities more accessible, organized, and transparent by connecting individuals seeking temporary work with job providers offering small-scale tasks.

The platform provides a centralized environment where users can publish job opportunities, create worker profiles, browse available jobs, and apply for opportunities that match their skills and qualifications.

EquiWork focuses on improving the recruitment process through skill-based job matching, predefined fair-wage validation guidelines, First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) application allocation, and persistent digital recordkeeping.

## 2. Problem Being Addressed

In the Philippines, informal and micro-job opportunities such as household errands, minor repairs, tutoring, cleaning, and other short-term services are commonly advertised through personal referrals, word-of-mouth communication, Facebook groups, and other informal networks.

These fragmented methods create several problems:

- **Fragmented Job Listings:** Job opportunities are scattered across different platforms and communication channels.
- **Poor Skill-to-Job Matching:** Workers and job providers have difficulty determining whether skills and qualifications match specific tasks.
- **Unclear Compensation:** Informal negotiations can result in inconsistent or unfair payment expectations.
- **Unfair Job Allocation:** Individuals without established personal connections may have fewer opportunities.
- **Lack of Accessible Records:** Job agreements, applications, and compensation details may be difficult to retrieve.

EquiWork addresses these challenges by providing a centralized and structured system for discovering, matching, and allocating informal job opportunities.

## 3. Project Objectives

### General Objective

To develop and deploy EquiWork, a Java-based informal job posting and matching system that improves accessibility to micro-job opportunities by centralizing job information and supporting skill-based matching, fair-wage validation, and transparent application allocation.

### Specific Objectives

1. Develop a centralized platform where job providers can publish available micro-jobs and informal workers can browse and apply for suitable opportunities.
2. Implement job search and filtering features based on job category, location, skills, and compensation.
3. Implement skill-based job matching, predefined fair-wage validation rules, and First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) application allocation.
4. Deploy the application online with continuous data storage.
5. Implement input validation, access controls, and safeguards for user information and job listings.
6. Evaluate the system through functional and user testing.

## 4. Target Users

EquiWork is designed for two primary user groups:

### Job Seekers / Informal Workers

- Individuals looking for temporary or short-term employment.
- Students seeking part-time or small-scale job opportunities.
- Individuals offering services such as tutoring, cleaning, repairs, and household assistance.
- Freelancers and individuals interested in micro-job opportunities.

### Job Providers

- Individuals looking for assistance with small-scale tasks.
- Households needing temporary services.
- Small businesses requiring short-term assistance.
- Individuals seeking workers with specific skills or qualifications.

## 5. Main Features

### 5.1 Centralized Job Posting

Allows job providers to publish opportunities with descriptions, categories, locations, required skills, and compensation.

### 5.2 Worker Profiles

Allows workers to register their skills and capabilities to support more relevant job matching.

### 5.3 Job Search and Filtering

Enables workers to browse and filter available opportunities according to relevant criteria, including category, location, skills, and compensation.

### 5.4 Skill-Based Job Matching

Matches or recommends available opportunities based on registered worker skills and the qualifications specified by job providers.

### 5.5 Compensation Validation System

Checks proposed job compensation against predefined system guidelines before a job posting is published. These guidelines serve as system references and are not official wage determinations.

### 5.6 First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) Application Allocation

Prioritizes qualified applicants according to their application submission timestamps, providing a consistent and transparent allocation process.

### 5.7 Digital Record System

Maintains records of job postings, applications, applicant statuses, and agreed compensation so users can retrieve relevant information beyond individual sessions.
